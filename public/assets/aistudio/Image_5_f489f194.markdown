## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a mission-critical, enterprise-grade governance environment synthesized from SAP Fiori Horizon and IBM Carbon design principles. Tailored specifically for compliance, identity governance, access request management (ARM / Solicitação de Acesso), and role maintenance (BRM / Manutenção de Função), the design language prioritizes operational clarity, audit-ready precision, and high-throughput data processing.

The aesthetic fuses crisp modern corporate engineering with functional minimalism. Density is balanced against deliberate breathing room to reduce cognitive friction across multi-step approval workflows, complex entitlement matrixes, and real-time security audit trails. Elements feel authoritative, stable, and deterministic, instilling operational confidence for enterprise risk managers, basis administrators, and compliance auditors alike.

## Colors
The palette leverages deep sapphire and authoritative indigo tones to construct visual hierarchy, balanced by an expansive neutral continuum engineered specifically for readability across complex analytical tables and structured data grids.

- **Primary (`#003865`)**: The core brand anchor, applied across high-level application chrome, global navigation bars, major module switches, and definitive structural headers.
- **Secondary (`#0F62FE`)**: The active enterprise action tint. Used for primary execution targets, focused navigation states, active step markers, and confirmed multi-select states.
- **Tertiary (`#0070F2`)**: System interactive accent. Powers secondary actions, interactive breadcrumbs, inline filter chips, and interactive data links.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B`)**: High-contrast slate charcoal governing typography, structural iconography, and baseline boundary definitions.
- **Surfaces & Canvases**: Base application canvas rests on clean cool-slate `#F4F6F9`, layered with pure white `#FFFFFF` component cards, panels, and data surfaces. Outlines and subtle column rules rely strictly on `#E0E4EC`.
- **Feedback & Semantics**: System feedback enforces unyielding compliance signaling:
  - **Success**: `#198038` (SAP Success Green) for approved delegations, valid role tests, and successful syncs.
  - **Warning**: `#F1C21B` / `#B28600` (Amber) for pending access, soD (Segregation of Duties) violations, and temporary privilege escalations.
  - **Critical / Danger**: `#DA1E28` for unauthorized conflicts, critical risk roles, and rejected requests.
  - **Information / Neutral Feedback**: `#0043CE` for operational hints and non-blocking system messages.

## Typography
Typography is constructed entirely on `Inter` for interface elements and tabular displays, backed by `JetBrains Mono` for system logs, SAP authorization objects, T-Code representations, and technical payloads.

- **Legibility & Tabular Alignment**: Numbers in tables, data metrics, and audit logs must enforce tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to maintain vertical scanning fidelity across numerical data.
- **Hierarchy Distinction**: Headlines utilize tighter negative tracking to maximize compact screen estate while retaining authority. Labels and section super-headers enforce uppercase letter-spacing for swift classification in dense form layouts.
- **Monospace Integration**: Security audit logs, SAP technical roles (e.g., `Z_BC_USER_ADMIN`), profile objects, and JSON payloads leverage `JetBrains Mono` at consistent line heights to prevent baseline jumping when interleaved with inline badges.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout system is founded on an 8pt architectural grid with a complementary 4pt micro-step system for compact enterprise form elements and dense data tables.

- **Grid Architecture**:
  - **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12-column fluid grid. Margin `2rem`, gutter `1.5rem`. Max container constraint for primary forms capped at `1600px` for optimal scanning.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout. Margin `1.5rem`, gutter `1rem`. Secondary analytical panels collapse into collapsible flyout drawers.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout. Margin `1rem`, gutter `1rem`. Tables switch into vertical key-value audit cards.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Layout margins around modules, form groupings, and dual-pane views use systematic scales (`space-md`, `space-lg`, `space-xl`). Compact elements such as action bars and table toolbars maintain high density with `space-xs` and `space-sm` gap tokens.

## Elevation & Depth
In harmony with SAP Fiori Horizon and Carbon's planar clarity, the design system minimizes expressive or theatrical drop shadows, relying on structural borders, tonal surfaces, and subtle ambient occlusion.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: System background `#F4F6F9`. No shadow, no outline.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface)**: Solid `#FFFFFF` fill enclosed by a crisp `#E0E4EC` 1px border. Ambient occlusion applied via `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Sticky Tab Rails, & Flyout Headers)**: Elevated white surface bordered by `#CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Role Comparison Drawers, Dropdown Panels)**: Elevated surface layered over backdrop scrim (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`). Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Level 4 (Floating Notification Toasts & Tooltips)**: Maximum elevation. Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`. Outlines maintain high contrast against active canvases.

## Shapes
A disciplined "Soft" curvature philosophy is applied across the system. UI elements default to `4px` (`0.25rem`), providing a modern, approachable edge while preserving structural geometric alignment critical for high-density grids and enterprise form controls.

- **Base Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Checkboxes, Chips)**: `4px` (`0.25rem`). Maintains the sharp, orderly appearance expected in administrative consoles.
- **Containers (Cards, Code Blocks, Module Panels)**: `8px` (`0.5rem`) via `rounded-lg` tokens, offering clean visual containment without wasted interior padding.
- **Dialogs & Overlay Surfaces**: `12px` (`0.75rem`) via `rounded-xl` for top-level floating context windows.
- **Pill Shapes (Exclusive to Status Badges & Counters)**: Complete full-radius `9999px` reserved strictly for status chips, numerical notification badges, and workflow counters to distinguish them visually from clickable inputs and actions.

## Components

### 1. Navigation & Module Switcher (ARM / BRM)
- **Top Segmented Tab Switcher**: High-contrast, dual-primary toggle distinguishing **ARM (Solicitação de Acesso)** and **BRM (Manutenção de Função)**. Active module displays a solid `#0F62FE` bottom border (3px) with `#003865` text; inactive module displays `#64748B` text over a subtle `#E2E8F0` hover background.
- **Sub-navigation / Step Wizard**: Horizontal chevron breadcrumb or numbered step indicator with validated check icons on completed phases.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#0F62FE`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none, 4px radius, height 36px (compact) or 40px (default). Hover: `#0043CE`. Focus: 2px offset ring in `#0F62FE`.
- **Secondary / Fiori Action**: White background, `#003865` border and text. Hover: `#F8FAFC` surface with `#001D6C` text.
- **Danger**: White background, `#DA1E28` border and text. Hover: `#DA1E28` background with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent background, text `#0070F2`. Hover: `#EFF6FF`.

### 3. Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text Inputs & Selects**: Height 36px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, typography `body-md`. Focus state uses a 1px border `#0F62FE` with a `2px` focus ring in `rgba(15, 98, 254, 0.2)`.
- **Validation**: Embedded inline messages below inputs with clear SVG glyphs (red cross for error, amber exclamation for SoD conflict warnings).

### 4. Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Precision 16x16px boxes with 2px borders. Selected states fill with `#0F62FE` displaying crisp white check glyphs. Indeterminate states for hierarchical role trees display a horizontal `#FFFFFF` bar.

### 5. Chips & Status Badges
- **Status Tags**: Pill shape (rounded-full), height 22px, padding `0 8px`, `label-sm` typography.
  - *Active / Approved*: `#DEFBE6` fill, `#198038` text.
  - *Pending / Risk Review*: `#FDF6B2` fill, `#8E4B10` text.
  - *SoD Conflict / Rejected*: `#FFE3E3` fill, `#DA1E28` text.
  - *Draft / Idle*: `#F1F5F9` fill, `#475569` text.
- **Removable Filter Chips**: Background `#E0F2FE`, text `#0070F2`, featuring an inline dismiss icon with immediate hit-target highlight.

### 6. Robust Data Cards
- Container card styled with `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#E0E4EC` border, and 8px corner radius. Includes a distinct 48px header strip with title, counter pill, and right-aligned contextual menu/action buttons. Card bodies utilize structured key-value grids (`label-md` muted above `body-md` bold values).

### 7. Code & Log Viewer Panels
- Dedicated dark/high-contrast console frame (`#0F172A` background) designed for authorization tracing, SAP ST01/SU53 simulation logs, and payload inspection.
- Line numbers styled in muted `#475569` with `code-sm` font.
- **Action Toolbar**: Floating top-right overlay containing a "Quick Copy" icon button with instant visual state feedback (icon toggles to a green checkmark for 2 seconds) and an "Export Raw" trigger.

### 8. Feedback Toasts
- Positioned in the bottom-right viewport (z-index: 1000). Constructed with 4px border radius, solid white surface, deep shadow (Level 4), and a definitive 4px left-edge semantic status stripe (`#198038` success, `#F1C21B` warning, `#DA1E28` critical). Includes explicit dismiss icon and auto-dismiss timing bar.
